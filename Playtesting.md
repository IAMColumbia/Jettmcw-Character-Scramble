# 3/3/2026 Playtesting Notes
## Student playtesters Tony, Nick, and Alex
- The game should telegraph what options are available to the left and right of the currently selected one, instead of just rapidly swapping.
- After a player's already finished the selection process, there could be a rudimentary way for players to move their created character around.
  - Would make it more clear that this was a "character selector."
  - Would give completed players something to play with while they wait for the other players to finish.
- The selected choices should be reversible -- currently, a choice is permanent with no way to back out.
- The ordering of choices should be random each time the game is played, to inhibit getting to accustommed to the way the game plays.
- The controls and objectives of the game are completely unclear to the players, needed to explain character-select premise.
- Player will instinctively press/mash buttons before going left/right, leading to them selecting immediately without cycling at all.

## David's Insights
- Concerns about positioning of player goals, perhaps it could be presented as a memory game where the objectives have to be remembered.
- The way a player could be composed could be done similarly to a certain minigame from Mario 3
- The concept could be extend to relate to poker hands, dealing the player different options
